Soundboard NG is an offline soundboard for theatre, live performances and events.

Features:
* Organize audio cues on multiple boards.
* Play, pause, resume, restart and loop sounds.
* Configure individual volume, tap behaviour and fade-in/fade-out.
* Choose whether multiple sounds may play simultaneously.
* Lock editing during performances.
* Export and restore portable ZIP backups, including audio files.
* Light, dark and system themes.

Free and open source under GPL-3.0. No ads, no tracking, no account and no network connection required.

Soundboard NG is a modified fork of Soundboard by 508.dev.
Source code: https://github.com/lattemacchiato/soundboard
